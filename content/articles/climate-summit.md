World leaders have achieved a historic agreement at the Global Climate Summit in Geneva.
§世界各国领导人在日内瓦全球气候峰会上达成了一项历史性协议。
After two weeks of intense negotiations, nearly 200 countries committed to reducing carbon emissions by fifty percent before the year 2035.
§经过两周的激烈谈判，近200个国家承诺在2035年之前将碳排放量减少50%。
The ambitious agreement represents a major advance in the international fight against climate change.
§这项雄心勃勃的协议标志着国际社会应对气候变化斗争的重大进展。

Scientists have repeatedly warned that without immediate and adequate action, global temperatures could rise by more than two degrees Celsius above pre-industrial levels.
§科学家们一再警告，如果不立即采取充分行动，全球气温可能比工业化前水平上升超过两摄氏度。
Such an increase would cause severe weather events, accelerate rising sea levels, and threaten agricultural production worldwide.
§这样的升温将导致极端天气事件、加速海平面上升，并威胁全球农业生产。
The scientific community has urged political leaders to adopt aggressive measures to address this growing crisis.
§科学界已敦促政治领导人采取积极措施应对这一日益严重的危机。

Developing nations will receive substantial financial aid to assist their transition to clean energy sources.
§发展中国家将获得大量财政援助，以帮助它们向清洁能源转型。
This fund, valued at one hundred billion dollars annually, will help countries build solar and wind power infrastructure and acquire the necessary technology.
§这项每年价值一千亿美元的基金将帮助各国建设太阳能和风能基础设施，并获取必要的技术。
International agencies will administer these resources and monitor progress to ensure accountability.
§国际机构将管理这些资源并监督进展，以确保问责。
The goal is to allow every nation to pursue sustainable development without sacrificing economic opportunity.
§目标是让每个国家都能在不牺牲经济机会的前提下追求可持续发展。

Environmental advocacy groups have cautiously welcomed the agreement but stress that successful implementation will be the true test.
§环保倡导团体谨慎地对该协议表示欢迎，但强调成功的实施才是真正的考验。
Previous climate accords have sometimes failed to deliver on their ambitious promises due to the absence of effective enforcement mechanisms.
§以往的气候协议有时因缺乏有效的执行机制而未能兑现其雄心勃勃的承诺。
These organizations argue that nations must be held accountable for meeting their stated targets.
§这些组织认为，各国必须为其达成的既定目标负责。

The next phase involves each country submitting a detailed national plan outlining specific measures to achieve their emission reduction targets.
§下一阶段，每个国家需要提交一份详细的国家计划，概述实现减排目标的具体措施。
Independent experts will review and assess these plans for accuracy and feasibility.
§独立专家将审查和评估这些计划的准确性和可行性。
Progress will be evaluated every two years to maintain momentum and transparency.
§每两年将对进展进行一次评估，以保持动力和透明度。
Young climate activists celebrated the announcement but acknowledged that continuing pressure will be essential to ensure that commitments translate into meaningful action.
§年轻的气候活动家们对这一声明表示庆祝，但也承认持续施压对于确保承诺转化为有意义的行动至关重要。
