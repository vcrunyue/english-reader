A landmark United Nations report has delivered an alarming assessment of the state of global biodiversity, warning that approximately one million animal and plant species now face the threat of extinction — many within mere decades.
§一份具有里程碑意义的联合国报告对全球生物多样性的状况发出了令人震惊的评估，警告称约有100万种动植物正面临灭绝的威胁——其中许多物种将在短短几十年内消失。
The accelerating rate of biodiversity loss is unprecedented in human history and threatens to destabilize the fundamental ecosystems that provide essential services supporting human civilization.
§生物多样性丧失的速度之快在人类历史上前所未有，并有可能破坏那些为人类文明提供基本服务的核心生态系统的稳定。

The primary drivers of this crisis are both well documented and deeply entrenched in modern economic systems.
§这场危机的主要驱动因素既有充分记载，也深植于现代经济体系之中。
Habitat destruction, driven primarily by agricultural expansion and urban development, remains the dominant factor.
§主要由农业扩张和城市发展驱动的栖息地破坏，仍然是主导因素。
Climate change has emerged as an increasingly significant threat, altering the environmental conditions to which species have adapted over millions of years of evolution.
§气候变化已成为一个日益严重的威胁，它改变了物种经过数百万年进化所适应的环境条件。
Industrial pollution, the spread of invasive species through global trade, and the unsustainable exploitation of natural resources all compound these pressures, creating a web of interacting threats that few species can withstand.
§工业污染、通过全球贸易传播的入侵物种以及不可持续的自然资源开发，都加剧了这些压力，形成了一张相互交织的威胁网络，很少有物种能够承受。

The consequences of biodiversity loss extend far beyond the disappearance of individual species.
§生物多样性丧失的后果远不止个别物种的消失。
Healthy, diverse ecosystems provide services that human societies absolutely depend upon: the pollination of food crops by insects and birds, the natural purification of water by wetlands and forests, the regulation of climate through carbon storage in forests and oceans, and the maintenance of soil fertility through complex biological processes.
§健康多样的生态系统提供了人类社会绝对依赖的服务：昆虫和鸟类对粮食作物的授粉、湿地和森林对水的自然净化、通过森林和海洋碳储存实现的气候调节，以及通过复杂生物过程维持的土壤肥力。
The aggregate annual economic value of these ecosystem services has been estimated at many trillions of dollars, dwarfing the global economy of many individual nations.
§这些生态系统服务的年度经济总值估计已达数万亿美元，使许多国家的经济总量相形见绌。

Indigenous communities, who act as stewards for a disproportionate share of the world's remaining biodiversity, are among the most directly and immediately affected by its continued decline.
§原住民社区守护着全球剩余生物多样性中相当大的一部分，他们也是最直接、最迅速地受到其持续减少影响的群体。
Their traditional ecological knowledge — accumulated over countless generations of close observation and interaction with local environments — represents an invaluable resource for conservation efforts.
§他们传统的生态知识——通过无数代人对当地环境的密切观察和互动积累而成——是保护工作中不可估量的宝贵资源。
Protecting indigenous land rights and supporting community-led conservation have proven to be among the most effective strategies for preserving biodiversity.
§保护原住民土地权利和支持社区主导的保护工作已被证明是保护生物多样性最有效的策略之一。

The report emphasizes that practical solutions exist but require fundamental and immediate changes across multiple sectors of society.
§报告强调，切实可行的解决方案是存在的，但需要在社会的多个层面进行根本性的、立即的变革。
Protecting and restoring natural habitats, transitioning to genuinely sustainable agricultural practices, substantially reducing industrial pollution, and aggressively addressing climate change are all essential components of an effective global response.
§保护和恢复自然栖息地、转向真正可持续的农业实践、大幅减少工业污染以及积极应对气候变化，都是有效全球应对措施的关键组成部分。
The authors stress that while the window for meaningful action is narrowing rapidly, it has not yet closed entirely.
§作者强调，虽然采取有意义行动的机会窗口正在迅速缩小，但尚未完全关闭。
The choices made by governments, corporations, and individuals in the coming decade will determine the biological legacy that current generations leave for all those that follow.
§政府、企业和个人在未来十年所做的选择，将决定当代人为子孙后代留下怎样的生物遗产。
