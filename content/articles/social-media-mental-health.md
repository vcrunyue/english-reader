A comprehensive new study has examined the complex relationship between social media use and mental health among young adults.
§一项全面的新研究考察了社交媒体使用与年轻人心理健康之间的复杂关系。
The findings paint a nuanced picture that challenges simplistic narratives about technology and psychological wellbeing.
§研究结果描绘了一幅微妙的图景，挑战了关于技术与心理健康的简单化叙事。
The research followed over five thousand participants aged eighteen to twenty-five over a three-year period, tracking their social media habits and regularly assessing symptoms of anxiety, depression, and social isolation.
§这项研究历时三年，追踪了五千多名十八至二十五岁参与者的社交媒体习惯，并定期评估他们的焦虑、抑郁和社交隔离症状。

The results reveal that the connection between social media and mental health is far from straightforward.
§结果显示，社交媒体与心理健康之间的联系远非直截了当。
Moderate use of social platforms was actually associated with several positive outcomes, including stronger social connections and reduced feelings of loneliness.
§适度使用社交平台实际上与一些积极结果相关，包括更强的社交联系和更少的孤独感。
Young adults who used these platforms primarily for direct communication with close friends reported better overall mental health than those who spent most of their time passively consuming content from strangers and influencers.
§主要使用这些平台与密友直接交流的年轻人，其整体心理健康状况优于那些大部分时间被动消费陌生人和网红内容的同龄人。
The apparent lesson is that the quality of online interactions matters substantially more than the raw quantity of time spent on these platforms.
§显而易见的教训是，在线互动的质量远比在这些平台上花费的纯粹时间重要得多。

However, the study also identified significant risks.
§然而，该研究也识别出了重大风险。
Heavy users who engaged in extended passive scrolling — consuming content without meaningful interaction — demonstrated increased levels of anxiety and lower overall life satisfaction.
§长时间被动刷屏——消费内容而没有有意义的互动——的重度用户表现出更高的焦虑水平和更低的生活满意度。
The constant exposure to carefully curated representations of others' lives appeared to foster unrealistic comparisons and feelings of inadequacy.
§持续接触他人精心修饰的生活展示，似乎助长了不切实际的比较和自卑感。
Participants who checked social media immediately upon waking and right before sleep showed the most pronounced negative effects.
§那些醒来后立即和睡前马上查看社交媒体的参与者，表现出了最显著的负面影响。

Experts argue that these findings highlight the urgent need for comprehensive digital literacy education rather than simplistic calls to reduce screen time.
§专家认为，这些发现凸显了全面数字素养教育的迫切需求，而非简单地呼吁减少屏幕使用时间。
Teaching young people to use social platforms in healthier, more intentional ways may be more effective than imposing arbitrary limits.
§教导年轻人以更健康、更有意识的方式使用社交平台，可能比施加武断限制更为有效。
The researchers emphasized that social media companies themselves bear significant responsibility for designing features that promote meaningful human connection rather than passive consumption.
§研究人员强调，社交媒体公司自身在设计功能方面负有重大责任，应促进有意义的人际连接而非被动消费。

The study also identified several protective factors that appear to buffer against negative outcomes.
§该研究还识别出几种似乎能缓冲负面影响的保护性因素。
Regular physical exercise, adequate sleep, and strong offline social connections all correlated with better mental health regardless of social media usage patterns.
§无论社交媒体使用模式如何，定期体育锻炼、充足睡眠和强大的线下社交联系都与更好的心理健康状况相关。
The researchers advocate for a holistic approach to mental health that considers the full range of factors affecting young adults rather than focusing narrowly on technology use alone.
§研究人员倡导一种全面的心理健康方法，综合考虑影响年轻人的各种因素，而非仅仅狭隘地关注技术使用本身。
