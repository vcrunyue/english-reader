Artificial intelligence is rapidly transforming the way students acquire knowledge and teachers administer instruction.
§人工智能正在迅速改变学生获取知识和教师管理教学的方式。
From adaptive learning platforms that analyze individual performance to automated grading systems that assist educators, AI tools are becoming increasingly abundant in classrooms around the world.
§从分析个人表现的自适应学习平台到协助教育工作者的自动评分系统，人工智能工具在全球各地的课堂上变得越来越普及。

One of the most significant developments is personalized learning software.
§最重要的进展之一是个性化学习软件。
These sophisticated programs assess a student's academic strengths and weaknesses, then adjust the difficulty of subsequent material accordingly.
§这些精密的程序会评估学生的学业优势与不足，然后相应地调整后续材料的难度。
When a student struggles with a particular abstract concept, the system acknowledges the gap and provides additional practice and alternative explanations.
§当学生在某个抽象概念上遇到困难时，系统会识别出这一差距并提供额外的练习和替代性的解释。
Conversely, when a student demonstrates absolute mastery, the software accelerates to more advanced content.
§相反，当学生表现出完全的掌握时，软件会加速推进到更高级的内容。
This approach has accomplished remarkable results in diverse educational settings.
§这种方法在多种教育环境中都取得了显著成效。

However, experts caution that AI should complement rather than replace human educators.
§然而，专家警告说，人工智能应该辅助而非取代人类教育者。
The role of teachers remains crucial in fostering creativity, critical thinking, and emotional intelligence — skills that machines cannot easily replicate.
§教师在培养创造力、批判性思维和情商方面仍然扮演着至关重要的角色——这些是机器难以轻易复制的技能。
Teachers also provide the encouragement and guidance that help students develop a positive attitude toward learning.
§教师还提供鼓励和指导，帮助学生培养积极的学习态度。
The appropriate balance between technology and human interaction is a topic of ongoing debate among educational authorities.
§技术与人际互动之间的适当平衡是教育当局持续争论的话题。

Privacy concerns accompany the widespread adoption of AI in education.
§隐私问题伴随着人工智能在教育领域的广泛普及。
Schools must ensure that student data collected by intelligent systems is adequately protected and used responsibly.
§学校必须确保智能系统收集的学生数据得到充分保护并负责任地使用。
Regulators in several countries are now developing comprehensive guidelines for the ethical use of AI in educational settings.
§多个国家的监管机构正在制定全面的指导方针，规范人工智能在教育环境中的道德使用。
These policies aim to prevent the abuse of sensitive information while allowing schools to take advantage of valuable technological resources.
§这些政策旨在防止敏感信息被滥用，同时允许学校利用宝贵的技术资源。

Despite these challenges, the potential benefits are substantial.
§尽管面临这些挑战，其潜在收益仍然巨大。
AI can help bridge educational gaps by providing quality learning resources to students in remote areas who lack access to experienced teachers.
§人工智能可以通过向缺乏优秀教师的偏远地区学生提供优质学习资源，帮助弥合教育差距。
The global academic community continues to advocate for equitable distribution of these technologies.
§全球学术界持续倡导这些技术的公平分配。
As AI tools evolve further, their impact on education is likely to expand dramatically, affecting every aspect of how future generations learn.
§随着人工智能工具的进一步发展，它们对教育的影响可能会急剧扩大，影响到未来几代人学习的方方面面。
