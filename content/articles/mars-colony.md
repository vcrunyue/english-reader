NASA has released its most comprehensive blueprint to date for establishing a permanent human presence on Mars, outlining a meticulously phased approach spanning two decades.
§美国国家航空航天局发布了迄今为止最为全面的在火星建立永久人类驻地的蓝图，概述了一个跨越二十年、精心分阶段的方案。
The document delineates specific technical milestones, resource requirements, and contingency protocols with unprecedented detail, signaling the agency's conviction that interplanetary colonization has transitioned from aspirational concept to engineering endeavor.
§该文件以前所未有的细节阐述了具体的技术里程碑、资源需求和应急方案，表明该机构坚信星际殖民已从理想化的概念转变为工程实践。

The plan directly confronts the fundamental challenges that have long confounded space colonization advocates.
§该计划直面长期以来困扰太空殖民倡导者的根本性挑战。
Radiation exposure — arguably the single gravest threat to long-term Martian habitation — would be mitigated through a combination of subterranean living quarters and active shielding technologies.
§辐射暴露——可以说是长期火星居住最严重的单一威胁——将通过地下生活区与主动屏蔽技术相结合的方式加以缓解。
Habitats would be strategically constructed within natural lava tubes, ancient underground caverns formed by volcanic activity billions of years ago.
§居住地将战略性建造在天然熔岩管内，这些古老的洞穴是数十亿年前火山活动形成的。
These geological formations provide inherent protection from both cosmic radiation and micrometeorite impacts, substantially reducing the structural demands on artificial shielding.
§这些地质构造能够提供对宇宙辐射和微陨石撞击的天然防护，大大降低了对人工屏蔽的结构要求。

In-situ resource utilization constitutes the cornerstone of the sustainability strategy.
§就地资源利用是可持续发展战略的基石。
The Martian atmosphere, although tenuous by terrestrial standards, contains abundant carbon dioxide that can be processed to produce both breathable oxygen and methane rocket fuel.
§火星大气虽然以地球标准来看十分稀薄，但含有丰富的二氧化碳，可以加工处理产生可呼吸的氧气和甲烷火箭燃料。
Subsurface ice deposits, whose existence has been corroborated by multiple orbital survey missions, would supply water for drinking, agricultural irrigation, and industrial processes.
§其存在已得到多次轨道勘测任务证实的地下冰层，将为饮用、农业灌溉和工业过程提供水源。
The plan emphasizes minimizing dependence on resupply missions from Earth, which face severe constraints imposed by planetary alignment launch windows occurring only every twenty-six months.
§该计划强调要尽量减少对来自地球的补给任务的依赖，因为这些任务受到每二十六个月才出现一次的行星对齐发射窗口的严格限制。

The psychological ramifications of extended isolation and confinement have received careful consideration in the habitat design specifications.
§长期隔离和封闭的心理影响在居住地设计规范中得到了仔细考量。
Dedicated social spaces, private quarters with customizable environments, and sophisticated communication infrastructure maintaining near-real-time connectivity with Earth are all incorporated despite the inherent signal delay.
§专门的社交空间、具有可定制环境的私人住所，以及维持与地球近乎实时连接的先进通信基础设施都被纳入设计，尽管存在固有的信号延迟。
Crew selection protocols will emphasize not only technical competence but also psychological resilience, emotional stability, and demonstrated interpersonal compatibility under conditions of prolonged stress.
§乘员选拔方案不仅强调技术能力，还强调心理韧性、情绪稳定性以及在长期压力条件下经证实的个人亲和力。

Skeptics have been quick to articulate reservations about the plan's ambitious timeline and technological assumptions.
§怀疑论者迅速对该计划雄心勃勃的时间表和技术假设表达了保留意见。
Many of the required systems — from reliable life support capable of operating for years without terrestrial resupply to radiation-hardened electronics — remain in nascent stages of development.
§许多所需系统——从能够在不依赖地球补给的情况下运行多年的可靠生命支持系统，到抗辐射电子设备——仍处于早期开发阶段。
The plan candidly acknowledges these uncertainties and identifies critical path items requiring concentrated research investment before proceeding to subsequent phases.
§该计划坦率地承认了这些不确定性，并确定了在进入后续阶段之前需要集中研究投入的关键路径项目。
Nevertheless, the document represents a conspicuous milestone in NASA's strategic evolution: a transition from abstract exploration objectives to concrete engineering specifications, from aspirational rhetoric to empirical planning, and from tentative proposals to detailed architectural frameworks.
§尽管如此，这份文件代表着NASA战略演进中的一个显著里程碑：从抽象的探索目标到具体的工程规范，从理想化的言辞到基于经验的规划，从初步的提议到详细的架构框架。
