Marine researchers have developed a groundbreaking technique for extracting microplastics from ocean water on a meaningful scale.
§海洋研究人员开发了一种开创性的技术，可以在有意义的规模上从海水中提取微塑料。
The innovative method employs a biodegradable compound that causes plastic particles to aggregate into larger clusters, making them substantially easier to capture and remove.
§这种创新方法使用一种可生物降解的化合物，使塑料颗粒聚集成更大的团块，从而更容易被捕获和清除。
This development addresses one of the most pervasive and intractable environmental challenges of the modern era.
§这一进展解决了现代时代最普遍、最棘手的环境挑战之一。

Microplastics — tiny particles often invisible to the naked eye — have been documented in the deepest ocean trenches, the most remote Arctic waters, and within the tissues of countless marine organisms.
§微塑料——这种肉眼通常看不见的微小颗粒——已在最深的海沟、最偏远的北极水域以及无数海洋生物的组织中被发现。
These particles enter the food chain when marine life mistakes them for nourishment, eventually accumulating in the fish and shellfish that humans consume.
§当海洋生物将它们误认为食物时，这些颗粒便进入食物链，最终在人类食用的鱼类和贝类中积累。
Scientists have acknowledged the potential health implications with growing alarm as evidence of widespread contamination accumulates.
§随着广泛污染的证据不断积累，科学家们以日益增长的警觉承认了潜在的健康影响。

The new technique, developed by a research team at the University of Tokyo, utilizes a natural polymer derived from sustainable seaweed sources.
§这项由东京大学研究团队开发的新技术，利用了一种从可持续海藻资源中提取的天然聚合物。
When introduced into contaminated water, this compound binds selectively with plastic particles, forming aggregates that can be removed using conventional filtration methods.
§当被引入受污染的水中时，这种化合物会选择性地与塑料颗粒结合，形成可以通过常规过滤方法去除的聚集体。
Initial laboratory tests have demonstrated removal rates exceeding ninety percent, with the binding compound itself biodegrading naturally within weeks.
§初步实验室测试显示去除率超过百分之九十，而结合化合物本身在数周内自然生物降解。
This autonomous breakdown addresses concerns about introducing additional synthetic materials into delicate marine ecosystems.
§这种自主分解解决了关于向脆弱的海洋生态系统中引入额外合成材料的担忧。

Scaling this technology to address the enormous scope of global ocean pollution remains a formidable challenge.
§将这项技术规模化以应对全球海洋污染的巨大规模仍然是一项艰巨的挑战。
The vast quantities of plastic waste entering marine environments annually would require industrial-scale deployment of the treatment process to achieve measurable impact.
§每年进入海洋环境的巨量塑料垃圾需要工业规模的工艺部署才能产生可衡量的影响。
However, researchers remain cautiously optimistic that the method can be adapted for integration into wastewater treatment facilities and coastal cleanup operations, where concentrated pollution makes intervention most feasible and cost-effective.
§然而，研究人员仍然持谨慎乐观态度，认为该方法可以适用于整合到废水处理设施和海岸清理作业中，在这些地方，集中的污染使干预最为可行且成本效益最高。

The economic dimension of this innovation is equally significant.
§这项创新的经济维度同样重要。
Previous approaches to ocean plastic removal have been prohibitively expensive, limiting their practical application.
§先前的海洋塑料清除方法成本过高，限制了其实际应用。
The relatively modest cost of the seaweed-derived polymer, combined with its biodegradable nature, makes this new approach substantially more viable for widespread adoption.
§海藻衍生聚合物相对适中的成本，加上其可生物降解的特性，使这种新方法在大规模推广方面更具可行性。
Environmental agencies and conservation organizations have expressed keen interest in supporting further development and field testing of the technology.
§环境保护机构和自然保护组织已表示出浓厚兴趣，愿意支持该技术的进一步开发和现场测试。
