Renewable energy is expanding at an unprecedented pace across the globe.
§可再生能源正在全球范围内以前所未有的速度扩张。
Solar and wind power installations have achieved record levels in nations on every continent, marking a dramatic shift in how the world generates electricity.
§各大洲国家的太阳能和风能装机容量均达到创纪录水平，标志着世界发电方式的巨大转变。
This transformation represents more than technological progress — it signals a fundamental change in our relationship with energy production and consumption.
§这一转变不仅仅是技术进步——它标志着我们与能源生产和消费关系的根本性变化。

The cost of solar panels has dropped by nearly ninety percent over the past decade.
§在过去十年中，太阳能电池板的成本下降了近百分之九十。
This dramatic decrease has made solar energy fully competitive with traditional fossil fuels in most major markets.
§这一急剧下降使太阳能在大多数主要市场上与传统化石燃料具备了完全竞争力。
In many regions, building new solar facilities is now cheaper than operating existing coal plants.
§在许多地区，建设新的太阳能设施现在比运营现有的燃煤电厂更便宜。
The economic advantage has accelerated adoption among both government agencies and private corporations, creating a positive cycle of investment and innovation.
§这一经济优势加速了政府机构和私营企业的采用，形成了投资与创新的良性循环。

Policy support has played an absolutely crucial role in this transition.
§政策支持在这一转型中发挥了绝对关键的作用。
Governments have adopted tax incentives, renewable energy targets, and direct subsidies that encourage large-scale investment in clean energy infrastructure.
§各国政府采取了税收激励、可再生能源目标和直接补贴等措施，鼓励对清洁能源基础设施的大规模投资。
The private sector has responded with enthusiasm, as major corporations commit to powering their entire operations with renewable electricity.
§私营部门也热情回应，大型企业承诺用可再生能源为其全部运营供电。
This partnership between public policy and private ambition has proven remarkably effective.
§公共政策与私营企业雄心之间的这种伙伴关系已被证明非常有效。

Energy storage technology is advancing rapidly to address one of the most significant criticisms of renewable power.
§储能技术正在迅速发展，以应对对可再生能源最重要的批评之一。
Advanced battery systems now allow solar and wind energy to be stored efficiently for use during periods when the sun is not shining or the wind is not blowing.
§先进的电池系统现在可以高效储存太阳能和风能，供无阳光或无风期间使用。
This breakthrough addresses the reliability concerns that previously limited the widespread adoption of intermittent energy sources.
§这一突破解决了先前限制间歇性能源广泛推广的可靠性问题。

Despite the remarkable progress, substantial challenges remain.
§尽管取得了显著进展，但重大挑战依然存在。
Building new transmission infrastructure to connect remote renewable installations with population centers requires massive investment and political will.
§建设新的输电基础设施以将偏远的可再生能源设施与人口中心连接起来，需要大量投资和政治意愿。
Regulatory frameworks in many countries have not kept pace with technological advances, creating administrative barriers.
§许多国家的监管框架未能跟上技术进步的步伐，造成了行政障碍。
However, the momentum behind the global energy transition appears unstoppable, with renewable capacity expected to double within the next five years.
§然而，全球能源转型背后的势头似乎不可阻挡，可再生能源装机容量预计将在未来五年内翻一番。
