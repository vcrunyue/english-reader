A team of physicists has achieved a significant milestone in quantum error correction, substantially advancing the timeline for practical quantum computing.
§一组物理学家在量子纠错领域取得了重要里程碑，大大推进了实用量子计算的时间表。
The breakthrough addresses one of the most intractable obstacles that has constrained this field for decades: the inherent fragility of quantum states that makes computational results unreliable.
§这一突破解决了数十年来制约该领域的最棘手障碍之一：量子态固有的脆弱性导致计算结果不可靠。

Quantum computers harness the counterintuitive principles of superposition and entanglement to perform calculations that would be infeasible for even the most powerful classical machines.
§量子计算机利用叠加和纠缠这些违反直觉的原理，执行即使最强大的经典计算机也无法完成的计算。
However, quantum states are exquisitely sensitive to environmental interference.
§然而，量子态对环境干扰极为敏感。
Thermal fluctuations, electromagnetic noise, and even cosmic radiation introduce errors that rapidly degrade computational fidelity.
§热涨落、电磁噪声甚至宇宙辐射都会引入错误，迅速降低计算保真度。
Previous approaches to mitigating these errors required such substantial overhead that practical quantum computing remained a distant aspiration.
§先前减轻这些错误的方法需要巨大的开销，使得实用量子计算仍然是遥远的愿望。

The new error correction protocol, developed collaboratively by researchers at MIT and ETH Zurich, demonstrates a threshold of fault tolerance that was previously considered unattainable with current-generation hardware.
§由麻省理工学院和苏黎世联邦理工学院的研究人员合作开发的这一新纠错协议，展示了此前被认为在当前硬件上无法达到的容错阈值。
By implementing a novel encoding scheme that distributes quantum information across a sophisticated lattice of physical qubits, the team achieved logical error rates that decrease exponentially as the number of physical qubits increases.
§通过实施一种将量子信息分布在复杂物理量子比特晶格上的新型编码方案，该团队实现了逻辑错误率随物理量子比特数量增加而呈指数级下降。
This represents a genuine paradigm shift in quantum architecture design.
§这代表了量子架构设计中的真正范式转变。

The practical implications are substantial and far-reaching.
§其实际意义是巨大且深远的。
Previous error correction methodologies required an impractical overhead of thousands of physical qubits to adequately protect a single logical qubit.
§先前的纠错方法需要数千个物理量子比特的不切实际的开销才能充分保护一个逻辑量子比特。
The new approach achieves comparable or superior protection with approximately one-tenth the resource requirements.
§新方法以大约十分之一的资源需求实现了相当或更优的保护。
This dramatic improvement substantially shortens the anticipated timeline for achieving quantum advantage — the point at which quantum computers can reliably outperform classical supercomputers on commercially relevant problems.
§这一显著的改进大大缩短了实现量子优势的预期时间表——即量子计算机在商业相关问题上能够可靠地超越经典超级计算机的时刻。

Industries ranging from pharmaceutical research to materials science and cybersecurity are monitoring these developments with keen interest.
§从药物研究到材料科学和网络安全等各个行业都在密切关注这些进展。
Practical quantum computing would revolutionize drug discovery by enabling accurate simulation of molecular interactions, transform materials engineering through precise modeling of atomic structures, and fundamentally alter the cybersecurity landscape by rendering many current encryption methods obsolete.
§实用量子计算将通过实现分子相互作用的精确模拟来革新药物发现，通过原子结构的精确建模来改变材料工程，并通过使许多当前加密方法过时而从根本上改变网络安全格局。
Venture capital investment in quantum technology startups has surged in response to these advances, with aggregate funding reaching unprecedented levels.
§风险资本对量子技术初创公司的投资因这些进展而激增，总融资额达到了前所未有的水平。
While fully fault-tolerant commercial quantum computers remain years away, this breakthrough has transformed the conversation from theoretical possibility to engineering timeline.
§虽然完全容错的商用量子计算机仍需数年时间，但这一突破已将其从理论可能性的讨论转变为工程时间表的规划。
